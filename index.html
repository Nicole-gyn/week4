Student Mental Health Data

Exploring the relationship between student lifestyles and mental health.

About the Dataset

This dataset contains information about students' demographic characteristics, daily habits and mental health scores.

The data includes age, gender, year of study, major category, sleep hours, screen time, physical activity, PHQ-9 scores and GAD-7 scores.

Research Questions

  • Is sleep duration associated with mental health scores?
  • Is daily screen time related to PHQ-9 and GAD-7 scores?
  • Is physical activity associated with mental health?

Data Limitations

This dataset can help explore possible patterns between lifestyle and mental health. However, associations do not prove causation. The results may not represent all students, and other factors may also influence mental health.
